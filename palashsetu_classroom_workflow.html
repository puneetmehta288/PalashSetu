🗂️ The 10 Core Features Grounded in Codebase

Classroom purpose, primary user action, and pedagogical student & teacher outcome for each capability.

Zero Cloud Dependency in Classroom
Feature 01 • Teacher Action authService

🔐 Local Profile & PIN Authentication

Classroom Purpose: Secures multi-teacher profile switching on shared rural tablets without requiring cloud accounts or cellular connectivity.

Key User Action: Teacher selects their profile name from list and enters a 4-digit PIN verified through local cryptographic SHA-256 hash.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Prevents accidental data overwrite between colleagues; immediately isolates grade roster and classroom preferences offline.
Feature 02 • Tribal Output LiveTranslation & Speech

🎙️ Classroom Live Voice Bridge

Classroom Purpose: Dissolves the linguistic barrier by instantly converting teacher's Hindi instructions into tribal mother tongue.

Key User Action: Teacher taps glowing microphone button and speaks natural instructional Hindi ("अपनी किताब खोलो", "सेबों को गिनो").
Student & Teacher Outcome: Deterministic sub-5ms translation produces Ol Chiki text and plays gentle 0.85x acoustic speech synthesis; tribal children comprehend without fear.
Feature 03 • Student Outcome Touch Visual Cards

👧 Student Tap-to-Respond Visual Cards

Classroom Purpose: Gives Class 1–3 indigenous children a voice to communicate back to non-tribal teachers with zero intimidation.

Key User Action: Children tap illustrated response cards: "I understood" (ᱦᱚᱭ ᱢᱟᱪᱮᱛ), "Repeat please" (ᱟᱨᱦᱚᱸ ᱞᱟᱹᱭ ᱢᱮ), or "May I answer?".
Student & Teacher Outcome: Tablet speaks polite Hindi audio back to the teacher, establishing two-way psychological safety and immediate formative feedback.
Feature 04 • Teacher Action Panchaadi Studio

📖 NIPUN Bharat Lesson Studio

Classroom Purpose: Delivers structured NEP 2020 Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education lessons across Balvatika to Class 3.

Key User Action: Teacher navigates through the 5-step Panchaadi pedagogy: Warm-up → Direct Instruction → Guided Practice → Independent Practice → Assessment.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Teacher gains structured classroom discipline without preparing external notes; students master foundational concepts via indigenous cultural analogies.
Feature 05 • Student Outcome FLN Visual Decks

🃏 FLN Visual Flashcards

Classroom Purpose: Accelerates early childhood phonics, shape recognition, and foundational numeracy through tactile multisensory cards.

Key User Action: Students explore 16 unlocked decks (96 vector cards), triggering 3D flip-and-reveal for dot counting grids, tens bundles, and native pronunciation taps.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Children internalize mathematical place value and phonemic sounds without relying on rote memorization.
Feature 06 • Teacher Action Worksheet Generator

🖨️ Dynamic Bilingual Worksheet Generator

Classroom Purpose: Provides screenless, pencil-and-paper student assessments and homework drills in resource-constrained schools.

Key User Action: Teacher customizes grade and drill type (randomized arithmetic drills, number matrices) and triggers 1-tap printable A4 bilingual PDF generation.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Students complete screenless desk drills with dual-language headers; teacher preserves tablet battery life for instruction.
Feature 07 • Tribal Output JCERT Dual-Reader

📚 JCERT Bilingual Textbook Reader

Classroom Purpose: Decodes official Jharkhand state syllabus textbooks directly into indigenous scripts and spoken dialects.

Key User Action: Teacher opens Grade 1–3 reader in dual-column layout (Hindi left, tribal script right), tapping words for inspect popups or audio read-alouds.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Children read along with paragraph audio; textbook becomes fully accessible instead of an incomprehensible barrier.
Feature 08 • Teacher Action Attendance & Cohorts

📋 Multi-Teacher Attendance & Linguistic Cohort Register

Classroom Purpose: Manages daily offline student roster while automatically tracking linguistic diversity (Santali, Ho, Mundari).

Key User Action: Teacher toggles Present/Absent/Late with dirty-state safety warnings and exports official Jharkhand e-Vidyavahini CSV format with 1-click.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Zero record loss during tablet shutdowns; teacher identifies language minority students at risk of dropout.
Feature 09 • Teacher Grievance ReportIssue & Queue

✍️ Teacher Grievance & Dialect Feedback Portal

Classroom Purpose: Empowers rural educators to report missing regional village terms and pronunciation nuances straight from the field.

Key User Action: Teacher logs missing vocabulary or wrong inflection in offline form with priority flags and optional contextual description.
Student & Teacher Outcome: Curriculum researchers receive grassroots linguistic feedback to continuously expand tribal vocabulary banks.
Feature 10 • Administrative Sync telemetryService

🔄 Store-and-Forward Telemetry & State Hub Sync

Classroom Purpose: Provides state-level literacy monitoring without requiring continuous internet in remote villages.

Key User Action: Local queue securely retains ≤1,500 records; when teacher attends weekly block meeting with internet, app auto-syncs to PalashCentralHub and purges sent records.
Student & Teacher Outcome: District officers view live FLN progress across tribal blocks without burdening rural teachers with manual paperwork.
